1k99 privacy policy

Updated on October 21, 2026

This policy covers every game published by 1k99 on Google Play. Our games have no login and no servers of their own: your progress stays on your phone. When a game has ads, they come from Google AdMob. When a game sells something, the purchase goes through Google Play. What each game uses is listed under Game by game.

Who we are

1k99 is an independent Android game studio. When this page says "we", it means 1k99. Contact: suporte1k99@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Each game saves your progress on the device itself: coins, unlocked items, missions, achievements, records, the player name you type and your settings. 1k99 does not receive this information. If you uninstall the game, your progress is deleted with it.

Ads

Ads in our games are provided by Google AdMob, Google's ad network. Depending on the game, they may be videos you choose to watch for a reward, ads shown between rounds or banner ads on screen. The Game by game section says which kinds each game uses.

To load and show an ad, the AdMob kit inside the game sends Google:

  • your connection's IP address, which gives a rough idea of your region;
  • technical device information, such as model, Android version and language;
  • the Android advertising ID, when the game is not marked as child-directed;
  • whether the ad was loaded, watched or tapped;
  • error reports from the ad kit itself.

Google uses this data to choose the ad, count views, pay the developer and prevent fraud. 1k99 only sees reports with totals, such as how many ads were watched in a day, and has no access to data about individual people.

In games for all ages, every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to content suitable for everyone. In those games Google shows only non-personalized ads and does not use the advertising ID.

In other games, where the law requires it (such as in the European Union and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. In any country, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, under Google and then Ads.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and read the Google Privacy Policy.

In-game purchases

When a game sells an item, the purchase goes through Google Play's payment system. Your card or other payment details stay with Google and never reach us. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase was approved and which item was bought, so it can deliver the item. Right now no 1k99 game sells anything.

Who the data is shared with

Only with Google: through AdMob for ads, and through Google Play for purchases. We don't sell data and we don't use any other analytics, tracking or social network services in our games. Everything sent is encrypted. If a new game uses another service, it will be listed on this page before launch.

Children

Our games don't ask for a real name, email, phone number, photo or exact location. The player name is just a nickname that stays on the phone. Even so, parents and guardians may guide children not to use their full name. Each game's age range is listed under Game by game.

Permissions

Our games use internet access and connection status to load ads and, when applicable, confirm purchases. They don't use the camera, microphone, contacts, files or GPS. If a game needs any other permission, it will be explained under Game by game.

How to delete your data

In games that offer it, open Settings and tap Delete progress. Uninstalling the game also deletes everything. Since we keep no copy of your progress, 1k99 has no data to return or delete. For ad data, use the Google options mentioned above.
